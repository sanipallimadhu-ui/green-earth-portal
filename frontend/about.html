About Us

World Eco Voice (ECOvoice) is a global NGO that focuses on reporting and addressing environmental pollution across the world. Our platform allows individuals, NGOs,and government bodies to report environmental issues through an advanced and interactive system.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a transparent environmental reporting platform that highlights the real impact of pollution and supports actions to mitigate climate change. We aim to unite people who love nature with professionals working for environmental protection.

Our Staff

Our team includes experienced professionals in climate science, public policy,social development, and environmental management. They have successfully implemented complex environmental solutions at state and national levels.
