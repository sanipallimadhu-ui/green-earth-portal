How our application helps to protect nature 🌱

🌫 Pollution Reporting

Users can report environmental pollution issues such as air, water, noise pollution and garbage problems easily through our platform.

📸 Photo Evidence Upload

The application allows users to upload photos as proof, which helps authorities understand the issue clearly.

📍 Location Identification

Users can provide the exact location of the environmental issue so that action can be taken faster and accurately.

🌳 Environmental Awareness

Our platform spreads awareness about protecting nature and encourages people to take responsibility for a clean and green environment.

📊 Issue Management (Future Scope)

Reported issues can be monitored and managed through an admin dashboard for better environmental control.
